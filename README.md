# Isombe: A Kitchen Puzzle Room

A first-person puzzle game in Unity 6. You have 6 minutes to cook **isombe** in a Rwandan kitchen. Solve five steps in order to open the dining room door and escape. If time runs out, the dish burns.

|---|---|
| Move | `W` `A` `S` `D` |
| Look | Mouse |
| Interact / pick up / place / put back | `E` or Left click |

## Puzzle tasks
1. **Gather:** find the four ingredients (some are in the fridge) and place them on the prep counter. The banana is a decoy.
2. **Pound:** pound the leaves in the mortar in time with the drum beat.
3. **Stove:** turn the knob until the flame is blue, then press the igniter.
4. **Cook:** add the bowls to the pot in the order shown by the coloured dots.
5. **Serve:** put the finished dish on the serving tray, then walk out through the door.
